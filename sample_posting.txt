Summer 2027 Private Wealth Management Intern
Example Wealth Partners, Seattle, WA

About the role
Our 10-week summer internship places students alongside financial advisors serving high-net-worth families. Interns support client meeting preparation, portfolio reviews, financial planning analysis, and prospecting research.

Responsibilities
- Prepare client review materials and portfolio performance summaries
- Assist with cash flow and retirement planning analysis in Excel and planning software
- Research prospective clients and market trends
- Support operations on account openings and asset transfers

Qualifications
- Rising junior or senior pursuing a degree in finance, accounting, economics, or related field
- Strong Excel and PowerPoint skills
- Excellent written and verbal communication; client-facing experience preferred
- Interest in pursuing the SIE and a career in wealth management

Application deadline: January 31, 2027
